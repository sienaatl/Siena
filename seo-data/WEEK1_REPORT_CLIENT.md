## What We Found and Why We Fixed It

We analyzed your Google Search Console data and found several patterns where your listings weren't converting views into clicks:

### Finding 1: Homepage Title Didn't Protect Brand Recognition

**What we saw:** Your old homepage title was "Mediterranean & Italian Restaurant in Alpharetta | Siena" - the brand name came last. Your brand searches ("siena restaurant alpharetta") perform excellently at 27.12% click rate, but the homepage overall was declining.

**Why it matters:** When your brand name is at the end of the title, it's less prominent in search results. People scanning the page might miss it. Since 86% of your clicks come from brand searches, protecting that performance is critical.

**What we changed:** New title: "Siena Restaurant | Italian Mediterranean Dining Alpharetta" - puts the brand first while keeping the descriptive keywords.

### Finding 2: Descriptions Weren't Showing Real Menu Differentiators

**What we saw:** Homepage description started with "Experience Siena, a..." which wastes space. Italian page description mentioned "Mediterranean twist" but didn't name specific dishes that make you unique.

**Why it matters:** Searchers scan descriptions in 2-3 seconds. Generic restaurant language ("experience", "chef-driven", "memorable dining") doesn't help them choose. Specific menu items they can't get elsewhere do.

**What we changed:** 
- Homepage now mentions "Handmade pasta, fresh seafood" (we verified these claims against your actual menu: house-made bucatini and rigatoni, plus calamari, octopus, salmon, branzino)
- Italian page now names specific dishes: "House-made rigatoni, bucatini & Italian classics" instead of generic "house-made pasta"

### Finding 3: Italian Restaurant Page Title Too Generic

**What we saw:** Title was just "Italian Restaurant in Alpharetta | Siena" (40 characters). No differentiator. When searchers see 10 results for "italian restaurant alpharetta", they all look similar.

**Why it matters:** Position 6 normally gets 3-5% of people clicking. You're at 0.37% - that means 92% fewer clicks than expected. The title needs to tell searchers why to choose Siena.

**What we changed:** Added "House-Made Pasta" to the title. Now it's "Italian Restaurant in Alpharetta | House-Made Pasta | Siena" (59 characters). This feature is verified in your menu and differentiates you from competitors who use dried pasta.

### Finding 4: No Structured Heading for Search Engines

**What we saw:** Your homepage had beautiful visual design with the "SIENA" text, but no actual H1 heading tag that search engines look for.

**Why it matters:** Search engines use H1 tags to understand page topics. Missing H1 means Google has to guess what the page is about.

**What we changed:** Added a hidden H1 tag (invisible to visitors, visible to search engines and screen readers): "Siena Restaurant - Italian Mediterranean Dining in Alpharetta, GA". No visual change to your design.

### Finding 5: Missing Internal Connections

**What we saw:** Italian restaurant page didn't link to your downtown restaurants page or date night page, even though searches like "downtown alpharetta restaurants" (642 times shown) and "date night alpharetta" (115 times shown) are relevant.

**Why it matters:** Internal links help Google understand how your pages relate to each other and give visitors pathways to relevant information.

**What we changed:** Added two natural links:
- After mentioning "124 Devore Rd" location, added link to downtown restaurants page
- In the date night section, added link to the dedicated date night page before the reservation button

### Finding 6: Accessibility and Image SEO Gaps

**What we saw:** 31 pages had missing alt text on decorative images (stars, dividers, icons).

**Why it matters:** Screen readers need alt text for visually impaired visitors. Search engines use alt text to understand images. Missing alt text hurts both accessibility and SEO.

**What we changed:** Added descriptive alt text to all images: "decorative star", "decorative divider", "location pin icon", "event planning icon".

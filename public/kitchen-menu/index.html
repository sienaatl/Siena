SIENA KITCHEN APP

Keep your kitchen menu one click away, in its own app window.

  1. Open this menu in Google Chrome.
  2. Click INSTALL APP above, or use Chrome’s install icon in the address bar.
  3. Confirm Install, then pin Siena Kitchen to your Dock or taskbar.

On a Mac, you can also open this menu in Safari and choose File → Add to Dock.
